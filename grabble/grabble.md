### 1. What’s the idea in one sentence?

> “A grid-based game where players compete to collect more apples than their opponent.”

---

### 2. What does the player actually do?

> “The player moves one step at a time on a grid, choosing left or right, and collects apples by landing on them.”

---

### 3. What’s the goal?

> “To collect more apples than the opponent by the end of the game.”

---

### 4. What makes it interesting or different?

> “Players can only move left or right—no backward or diagonal movement—forcing strategic decisions and limiting escape options.”

---

### 5. What’s the simplest version of this?

> “A single-player version where the player moves left or right on a grid and collects apples without an opponent.”
